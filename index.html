Buko Pandan

My take on the Filipino party classic — creamy, cold, and so refreshing. Young coconut and bright green pandan jelly folded into sweet cream. One spoon and it tastes like home.

  • Young coconut (buko) strips in sweet cream
  • Pandan jelly cubes for that signature green
  • Served ice-cold in a glass, ready to dig in

About the cook

Hi, I’m Jiji

Jeraldine “Jiji” Masamong

I’m a Filipino cook with seven years of kitchen experience. I learned to love food back home in the Philippines, then built my career in kitchens across the United States — from Walt Disney World in Florida to Eddie’s Cafe in West Glacier, Montana, where I ran the kitchen when the manager and sous chef were away, created specials, and trained new staff.

At home, I cook the way my heart remembers: homemade, generous, and full of flavor. This page is where all of it lives.

My dream is to one day open my own restaurant or food truck — and every dish here is a step toward it.
